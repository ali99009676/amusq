## ملاحظات للمراجع (App Review Information → Notes)
Muraja ("مراجعة") is a question-bank study app for Arabic-speaking university students.

How to review without an account: tap "تصفّح كزائر" (browse as guest) on the login screen — free subjects, the mock exam, explanations and the leaderboard are all available.

Sign in options: email magic link + 6-digit code, Sign in with Apple, Google, GitHub. A demo account is not required; if you prefer one, use the email code flow with any address you control.

This version has no in-app purchases. Paid subjects are not offered inside the app (no prices, no external purchase links). Account deletion: حسابي → الحساب → حذف الحساب (Account → Delete account) — immediate and permanent.

Offline: subjects opened once are cached on device; the app keeps working without a connection.
